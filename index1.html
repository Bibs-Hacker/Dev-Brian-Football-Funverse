Community guidelines

Terms & Conditions

1. Fan-made content

FOOTBALL FANVERSE creates unofficial supporter cards for personal enjoyment. Cards are not official club credentials, proof of membership, tickets, identity documents, or endorsements.

2. Respect & ownership

Only upload photos and text you have the right to use. Do not post abusive, unlawful, impersonating, hateful, or privacy-invasive content. You remain responsible for the content you submit.

3. Privacy and visibility

Cards are private by default. A card saved locally stays in that browser. If cloud saving is enabled, private cards are associated with your account and should be accessible only to you. Feedback you submit to the community is public and may show your chosen display name.

4. Account security

Use a strong password and keep your login details private. Do not share secrets or payment information in feedback. We will never ask for a service-role key or password in public comments.

5. Community reactions

Likes and hearts are for respectful feedback. Attempts to spam, manipulate ratings, or disrupt the service may be restricted.

6. Availability

This fan project is provided for entertainment and may change. Download your own copies of cards you want to keep. The service does not promise official club data, club affiliation, or uninterrupted availability.

7. Contact and updates

Terms may be updated as the project grows. Continuing to use the service after an update means you accept the updated terms.
